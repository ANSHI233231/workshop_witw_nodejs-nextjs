# Guest

**What it is.** Someone on the website who is not signed in. There is no account and no role.

**Who uses it.** Any visitor of the website (`apps/web`).

## What they can do

- Browse shared safaris, filter them, open a safari, read its discussion and see who is interested ([WITW Shared Safari workflow](../workflows/WITW-Shared-Safari-Workflow.md)).
- Browse packages and parks, filter and sort them, open a package or a park page ([WITW Packages workflow](../workflows/WITW-Packages-Workflow.md)).
- Sign Up, or Become a Partner with new account details (the account is made and signed in with the request).

## What they cannot do

- Join a safari, comment, flag or organise: Join takes them to Login; the Organise button is not shown.
- Request a quote: the package page shows a sign-in prompt whose Login comes back to the package.
- Open My Shared Safaris, My Quote Requests or Notifications: they are sent to Login.

## How someone becomes one

- Nobody is made a guest: anyone signed out is one.
